NitMod 2.3.5 - GOOMBA (landing on a player to hurt / kill them)
===============================================================
Behavioural analysis of the shipped NitMod 2.3.5 binaries (qagame.mp.i386.so and
cgame.mp.i386.so) with Ghidra via REA. Nothing was changed. This describes behaviour,
not code to copy.


WHERE IT LIVES
--------------
The landing event handler (ClientEvents). The same logic is duplicated in G_FallDamage.


WHEN IT TRIGGERS
----------------
It hooks the player's landing events. Stock ET only reacts to damaging falls; NitMod
also reacts to EV_FALL_FAR, the hardest landing that does no fall damage, so smaller
drops can goomba too. Each landing has a base value = its normal fall damage:

  Landing                 Base   Stun (normally on the jumper)
  EV_FALL_FAR (no dmg)    5      -
  EV_FALL_DMG_10          10     250 ms
  EV_FALL_DMG_15          15     250 ms
  EV_FALL_DMG_25          25     500 ms
  EV_FALL_DMG_50          50     1000 ms
  EV_FALL_NDIE (deadly)   500    -

(The stun is pm_time + PMF_TIME_KNOCKBACK: the landing slowdown.)


WHO WAS LANDED ON
-----------------
- First the entity the game says you are standing on (ground entity).
- If that is not a player, a trace 4 units straight down from your feet (solid, bodies,
  corpses; ignoring yourself) and whatever player that hits.
- The victim must be a player who can take damage.


WHAT HAPPENS (g_goomba not 0 and a victim found)
------------------------------------------------
1. Team check: with flag 1, landing on a teammate does nothing at all - no goomba and
   no fall damage for the jumper either. Skipped in gametype 8 (a hidden mode beyond
   Team Death Match = 7).
   For no-damage landings only: flag 2 = no goomba at all, flag 4 = none on teammates.
2. The victim gets the jumper's stun (landing slowdown) and its pain sound is held back.
3. Damage to the victim, credited to the jumper as MOD_GOOMBA, only if the jumper is alive:
     normally       g_goomba x base
     flag 16        the victim's whole remaining health, ignoring spawn protection and
                    god mode = instant kill
4. The jumper:
     no-damage landing (base 5)  takes nothing; a goomba sound event plays at the victim
     damaging landing            takes 20% of the normal fall damage (nothing with flag 8)

If g_goomba is 0 or there is no victim: normal fall damage and stun for the jumper.


CVARS
-----
g_goomba        default 10   damage multiplier on the landing's base; 0 = off
g_goombaFlags   default 25 (= 1 + 8 + 16)
                  1   never on teammates (except gametype 8)
                  2   no goomba from no-damage landings, only from damaging falls
                  4   no goomba on teammates from no-damage landings
                  8   the jumper takes no fall damage
                  16  instant kill


REWARDS AND DISPLAY
-------------------
- A goomba kill gives the jumper +5 Battle Sense XP.
- A per-player goomba counter, shown in the stats as "Goomba Kills: %d".
- "First Goomba Kill" global announcement and the end-of-map award
  "Fat ass (most goombas)".
- Obituary: "was stomped by".


NOTES FOR A DIRTY DOUBLE JUMP PORT
----------------------------------
- ET: Legacy's pmove never sends EV_FALL_FAR: below the damage height (delta 38.75) it
  only sends EV_FALL_SHORT, so a port needs its own minimum height for a no-damage
  goomba. NitMod's exact threshold for that landing was not checked.
- ET: Legacy's fall code has no landing stun and gibs on NDIE, so the stun hand-over and
  the 500 base would be new behaviour.
