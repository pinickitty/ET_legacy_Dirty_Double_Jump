ET: Legacy - Dirty Double Jump (mod folder: legacyddj)
=====================================================

A server/client mod built on ET: Legacy 2.86.2. Only the mod libraries (qagame, cgame, ui)
and the mod pk3 change; the official ET: Legacy 2.86.2 executables are used as they are.
This file is copied into the pk3 as DirtyDoubleJump_<version>.txt for every build.

Version names: releases are git tags v2.86.2-ddj<major>.<minor> (current release v2.86.2-ddj1.3).
A release build shows that name in the console and serverinfo (mod_version) and is packed as
legacy_v2.86.2-ddj1.3.pk3. Test builds made after a release show the commits since it, e.g.
v2.86.2-ddj1.3-3-g1a2b3c4, and are packed as legacy_v2.86.2-ddj1.3_dirty.pk3.

1. CODE CHANGES (compared with stock ET: Legacy 2.86.2)
-------------------------------------------------------

Air jump (double jump)
  - NitMod-style second jump in the air, off by default (g_airJump).
  - Behaviour matches NitMod 2.3.5's double jump; the height is g_airJumpScale x a normal jump.

Tripmine (engineer)
  - New weapon WP_TRIPMINE (56), weapon bank 7 after landmine/adrenaline. Engineers spawn with
    one when tripmines are enabled (g_tripmines 1, or g_weapons 32768).
  - Placed on a wall, armed with pliers like a landmine; the laser goes live g_tripmineArmTime ms
    after "Tripmine armed" so the engineer can step out of it. Anyone crossing the beam sets it
    off (see the g_friendlyFire bits below for teammates and the owner).
  - Armed tripmines per team are limited by team_maxTripmines; a player's tripmines are removed
    when he leaves or changes team (g_tripmineTimeout 1).
  - With the tripmine selected, the HUD ammo counter shows how many tripmines the team can still
    arm (team_maxTripmines minus the team's armed tripmines), like the landmine counter.
  - Enemy tripmines show up with Battle Sense trap awareness like landmines, and can be defused
    with pliers. They can be shot and destroyed with g_damageweapons 32.
  - Model and textures from NitMod 2.3.5 (credited in models/multiplayer/tripmine/CREDITS.txt).
  - Obituaries: "tripped over X's Tripmine", "forgot where his tripmine was".

Poison syringe (needle)
  - New weapon WP_POISON_SYRINGE (57), weapon bank 5 after the medic syringe. Given at spawn
    (or straight away when the Light Weapons level is reached) to players allowed by g_poison,
    g_poisonClasses and g_poisonLevel. 4 injections (1 loaded + 3), up to 8 with ammo packs,
    500 ms between injections.
  - An injection at revive range poisons a living player: 1 HP every 50 ms (or 10 HP every
    1.5 s with g_poison 4). A second injection stacks and multiplies the damage. The victim
    gasps and a green poison icon is shown over poisoned players to everyone.
  - Refused (and the injection refunded): misses, dead players, spawn-protected players, and
    teammates unless g_poison has bit 16.
  - Poison kills are credited to the poisoner ("was poisoned by"). Poison ticks give no hit
    sounds, no screen blood and no view kick. Dying, respawning or being revived ends it.
  - Cures: a medic syringe on a poisoned teammate; health packs and health cabinets with
    g_poison 2 (with g_poison 8 not the poisoner's own packs).
  - /kill is refused while poisoned. /poison and /poison cure: cheat command for testing.
  - Skins/textures and the poisoned sprite from NitMod 2.3.5 (credited in
    models/multiplayer/syringe/CREDITS_poison.txt).

Other fixes and NitMod-compatible options
  - Re-pressing a weapon bank key now steps one weapon at a time (it skipped every other slot,
    so bank 7 could jump over the tripmine).
  - Spotted enemy mines can be drawn red (Axis) / blue (Allies) with g_misc 16.
  - Landmines can use a precise "touch" trigger (n_preciseLandmineTrigger 1).
  - A friendly fire vote only switches bit 1 of g_friendlyFire and keeps the other bits.
  - The DEVELOPMENT BUILD marker is hidden and the mod name is coloured.
  - The mod installs into its own folder (legacyddj) with working translations.


2. NEW CVARS
------------

g_airJump               0     0 = off, 1 = air jump any time, 2 = only while still rising
g_airJumpScale          1.4   air jump velocity as a multiple of a normal jump

g_tripmines             0     1 = engineers get a tripmine (latched: needs a map restart)
team_maxTripmines       5     armed tripmines allowed per team (NitMod default 5)
g_tripmineTimeout       1     1 = remove a player's tripmines when he leaves or changes team
g_tripmineArmTime       3000  ms between "Tripmine armed" and the laser going live (min 250)
g_tripmineDebug         0     1 = tripmine debug log in the server console, 2 = also the pliers trace

g_poison                0     poison syringe bitflags, 0 = off:
                                1  poison on: the poison syringe; the medic syringe cures poison
                                2  health packs and health cabinets also cure poison
                                4  slow poison: 10 HP every 1.5 s instead of 1 HP every 50 ms
                                8  health packs from your own poisoner don't cure you
                                16 teammates can be poisoned (default: never, whatever g_friendlyFire is)
g_poisonClasses         0     classes that get the poison syringe, add the values:
                                1 soldier, 2 medic, 4 engineer, 8 field ops, 16 covert ops
                                0 = all classes (e.g. 2 = medics only, 18 = medics and covert ops)
g_poisonLevel           4     Light Weapons level needed for the poison syringe (0 = everyone)

g_weapons               0     NitMod-compatible bitflags: 32768 = tripmines (same as g_tripmines 1)
g_damageweapons         0     NitMod-compatible bitflags: 32 = tripmines can be shot and destroyed
n_preciseLandmineTrigger 0    1 = a landmine only goes off when your bounding box touches it


3. CHANGED CVARS
----------------

g_friendlyFire          only bit 1 turns friendly fire on now, so these NitMod bits can be added:
                          1   friendly fire on (all weapons)
                          32  teammates don't set off your team's landmines
                          64  landmines and tripmines hurt teammates even with friendly fire off
                          128 teammates don't set off your team's tripmines
                          256 you can't set off your own landmines or tripmines
                          512 landmines and tripmines never hurt teammates or their owner,
                              even with friendly fire on
                        examples: 929 = 1+32+128+256+512 (FF on, mines safe for your team),
                                  417 = 929-512 (FF on, mines hurt teammates)
g_misc                  now in serverinfo so clients can read it; new bit 16 = spotted enemy
                        mines drawn red/blue instead of white
g_disableComplaints     new bit 8 = no complaints for tripmine teamkills


4. NEW COMMANDS
---------------

/poison [cure]          cheat (devmap / sv_cheats): poison yourself, or cure it
